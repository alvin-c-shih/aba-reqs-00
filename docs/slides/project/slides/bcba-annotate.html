Annotate without leaving the graph

Hover a session and click the plus, or press N. Type, press Enter.

Notes sit in a quiet lane under the axis, so the data stays clean.

Three kinds of note: a plain note, a treatment change, and context such as a fire drill or a substitute teacher. Each has its own marker shape in the lane. Saving shows a message with Undo instead of asking for confirmation first.
