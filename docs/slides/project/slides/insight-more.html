Four more signals, each with its reason

An unusual session gets a ring and an offer to add context.

Flags for recorders who score differently, and for prompt dependence.

Recorders differ: two people recording the same program with consistently different results, an early sign of scoring drift. Prompt-dependent: independent responding is flat while the share of prompted trials climbs. Thresholds are engineering defaults and need a BCBA to review them against real data.
